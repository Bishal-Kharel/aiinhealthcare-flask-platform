📚 Dataset & Model Sources

1. MedQuAD (Medical Question Answering Dataset)

  • Source: MedQuAD - NIH
  • License: Public Domain (U.S. Government Work)
  • Description: Over 45,000 medical Q&A pairs from NIH covering a wide range of health topics.
  • Format: XML (parsed into Q&A text)
  • Usage: Used in a vector database for retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) by our AI Health Assistant.

2. 3D Medical Model

  • Source: NIH 3D Model - Entry #21626
  • License: CC-BY (Creative Commons Attribution)
  • Description: Anatomically accurate model used for enhanced visual engagement in the AI interface.
